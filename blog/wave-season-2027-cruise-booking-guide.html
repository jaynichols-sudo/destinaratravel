Wave Season 2027: When to Book Next Year’s Cruise (and What You’ll Get)

By Jay Nichols • October 2, 2026 • 8 min read

Ask anyone in the cruise industry about the best time to book and you’ll hear two words: wave season. From roughly January through March, cruise lines roll out their strongest promotions of the year to fill ships for the coming summer, fall, and beyond. For 2027 sailings, that window opens in just a few months.

But here’s what most travelers don’t realize: waiting for wave season isn’t always the smartest move. The best cabins on the most popular itineraries often sell before January ever arrives. This guide covers what wave season really offers, who should book now, and how we protect our clients’ fares either way.

What Is Wave Season?

Wave season is the cruise industry’s busiest booking period, traditionally running from early January through the end of March. After the holidays, travelers start planning the year ahead, and cruise lines compete for those bookings with promotions you rarely see at other times of year.

The name is simple: it’s the “wave” of bookings that floods in at the start of every year. For travel advisors like us, it’s the busiest stretch on the calendar.

What Kinds of Perks Show Up During Wave Season?

Every line and every year is different, and specific offers are announced by the cruise lines themselves. But these are the types of promotions we typically see:

  • Reduced deposits — Lock in a cabin with a smaller up-front payment.
  • Onboard credit — Spending money for specialty dining, spa treatments, or shore excursions.
  • Included extras — Beverage packages, Wi-Fi, gratuities, or specialty dining bundled into the fare.
  • Cabin upgrades — Move up a category (interior to ocean view, balcony to suite) at little or no cost.
  • Third and fourth guests at reduced fares — A big win for families sharing a stateroom.
  • Air credits or air-inclusive fares — Especially common with luxury and river lines.
“The best wave season offer is the one on the ship, the itinerary, and the cabin you actually want. A great perk on the wrong cruise is no deal at all.”

Who Should Book Right Now

  • Alaska travelers — The season is short (May through September) and the best balcony cabins on the most scenic Inside Passage itineraries go early.
  • Families traveling in summer 2027 — Connecting cabins and family suites are limited on every ship, and school-break sailings fill first.
  • Suite guests — There are only a handful of top-tier suites on any ship. If that’s your style, don’t wait.
  • Groups, weddings, and reunions — Securing a block of cabins together is far easier a year out. (Our group travel team handles this every week.)
  • European river cruises — River ships are small. Popular spring tulip sailings and the holiday Christmas market cruises sell out well in advance.

Who Can Wait for Wave Season

  • Flexible travelers — If you’re open on dates, ships and cabin categories, wave season can deliver real value.
  • Caribbean and Bahamas cruisers — With so many ships sailing these waters year-round, availability is rarely an issue.
  • First-time cruisers still deciding — Use the fall to figure out which style suits you. Our river vs. ocean cruise guide is a good place to start.

How We Protect Your Fare Either Way

The biggest worry we hear is, “What if I book now and the price drops in January?” It’s a fair question, and it’s exactly where working with an advisor pays off.

  • We watch your booking. When eligible fares drop or a new promotion applies before final payment, we request the adjustment on your behalf wherever the cruise line’s policy allows.
  • We explain the fine print. Refundable vs. non-refundable deposits, final payment dates, and promotion eligibility vary by line. We spell it out before you commit.
  • We stack what we can. Advisor-exclusive amenities can often be added on top of cruise line promotions.
  • We spread the cost. Ask about FlexPay financing to pay for your cruise in monthly installments.

Your 2027 Cruise Planning Timeline

  • October–December 2026: Decide on destinations and travel dates. Book Alaska, summer Europe, suites and group sailings.
  • January–March 2027: Wave season. Watch for perks on flexible plans and Caribbean sailings, and ask us to check existing bookings for new offers.
  • 60–120 days before sailing: Final payment is typically due. Lock in shore excursions, specialty dining and spa appointments.
  • 30 days out: Online check-in, travel documents, and a final review of your packing checklist.

The Bottom Line

Wave season is real, and the perks can be excellent. But the right time to book is when the cruise you want is still available at a price that works for you. For many 2027 sailings, that time is now.
