Best Honeymoon Destinations for 2027

By Elizabeth Nichols Updated October 2026 14 min read

Your honeymoon is one of the most meaningful trips you'll ever take — and couples planning for 2027 are seeking something beyond the expected. After years of planning dream honeymoons for our clients, Elizabeth and I have identified five destinations that are trending right now for all the right reasons: breathtaking scenery, unmatched romance, exclusive experiences, and service that makes you feel like the only two people on Earth.

This guide is updated for couples marrying in late 2026 and 2027. Start with our five favorites, then use the season-by-season guide, flight times from the East Coast, honeymoon perks, registry tips, and FAQ to narrow it down.

Destination 01

The Maldives — The Gold Standard of Romance

The Maldives remains the undisputed honeymoon capital of the world — and the islands keep raising the bar. We're seeing our couples choose private island resorts with dedicated butler service, underwater dining experiences, and sunrise paddleboarding over crystal-clear lagoons. The trend this year is booking villa-only resorts where you'll never share a pool or beach with more than a handful of guests.

Resorts like Soneva Fushi, Gili Lankanfushi, and Cheval Blanc Randheli offer jaw-dropping overwater villas with private pools, outdoor showers under the stars, and direct lagoon access. Sunsets here aren't just beautiful — they feel personal, like the island is putting on a show just for you.

Why couples choose the Maldives
  • New ultra-private villa resorts opening on uninhabited atolls
  • New Maldives air connections from the US via Doha and Dubai
  • Best value in the dry season: December–April travel window
  • Destinara can secure complimentary room upgrades & resort credits

Destination 02

Santorini, Greece — Iconic for a Reason

There's a reason Santorini tops every honeymoon list — it genuinely delivers. The caldera views from Oia and Imerovigli are unlike anything else in the world, and the combination of cave hotels carved into volcanic rock, volcanic sand beaches, and world-class Greek cuisine makes every moment feel cinematic. For 2027 honeymoons, we're recommending couples arrive in late April or early October to enjoy the romance without the peak-summer crowds.

The savviest honeymooners are now pairing Santorini with a night or two in Athens (for the Acropolis and the buzzing food scene) and sometimes extending into Mykonos for a contrasting energy. The Greek island-hop honeymoon is one of our most requested itineraries this year.

Destinara's Santorini picks
  • Katikies Hotel — infinity pool with caldera views, adults-only
  • Grace Hotel Santorini — elegant, cliff-side, with Champagne sunsets
  • Private sunset sailing catamaran with onboard dinner
  • Wine tasting tour through Assyrtiko vineyards in Pyrgos

Destination 03

Bali, Indonesia — The Wellness Honeymoon

Bali is having a massive resurgence as a honeymoon destination, and the couples choosing it are decidedly different from the backpacker crowd of a decade ago. Today's Bali honeymoon means a private villa in Ubud with your own plunge pool surrounded by jungle, couples' Balinese massage and flower-petal bath rituals, sunrise hikes up Mount Batur followed by breakfast in the clouds, and romantic rice-terrace dinners lit by torchlight.

The luxury villa scene in Bali is genuinely world-class — at a fraction of what you'd pay in the Maldives or Santorini. A 7-night Bali honeymoon in a stunning private villa with daily breakfast and a personal driver costs far less than you'd imagine, leaving room in the budget for incredible experiences.

Bali experiences couples love
  • Private Chef dining experience in your villa garden
  • Couples' healing ceremony with a traditional Balinese healer
  • White-water rafting through the Ayung River gorge
  • Temple tour at Tanah Lot at sunset with a local guide
💡

Advisor Tip from Elizabeth: One of the most overlooked honeymoon upgrades is a "room drop" — a surprise in-room setup arranged secretly through the hotel. Think rose petals, champagne on ice, personalized notes, and floating candles in the bath. We arrange these complimentary for all our honeymooners through our preferred partner properties. It's the detail that makes couples cry (happy tears!) when they walk into their room the first night.

Destination 04

Amalfi Coast, Italy — La Dolce Vita Honeymoon

The Amalfi Coast is consistently one of our top sellers for honeymooners, and that hasn't changed heading into 2027. What makes it irresistible: dramatically beautiful cliffside scenery, exceptional food and wine, charming villages like Ravello and Positano, and a pace of life that makes you forget time exists. Couples who choose Italy often tell us it was "the most beautiful place we've ever been" — and we tend to agree.

The ideal Amalfi honeymoon combines a few nights in a clifftop villa in Ravello (the quieter, more romantic alternative to Positano), a private boat day cruising the coastline and stopping at hidden sea caves, and a day trip to Pompeii for couples who want a touch of history. Pair it with a night or two in Rome before or after, and you have one of the great honeymoon itineraries in the world.

Must-do experiences
  • Private boat charter along the coast with onboard aperitivo
  • Limoncello making class in a hillside lemon grove
  • Dinner at La Sponda, Positano — candles everywhere, unforgettable
  • Sunrise walk along the Path of the Gods above the clouds

Destination 05

St. Lucia — The Caribbean's Most Romantic Island

Among Caribbean islands, St. Lucia stands apart for honeymooners — it's dramatic, lush, and inherently romantic in a way that beach-flat islands simply aren't. The Piton volcanic peaks rising from the sea, the rainforest zip-lines, the muddy sulphur springs, and the incredible dive sites make it feel adventurous and exotic even within a 4-hour flight from the East Coast. St. Lucia has become our go-to recommendation for couples who want the Caribbean but want more.

Jade Mountain Resort — with its open-fourth-wall "Sanctuary" suites overlooking the Pitons — is genuinely one of the most spectacular hotel experiences on the planet. Sugar Beach and Anse Chastanet are equally spectacular alternatives. All three offer honeymooner packages with dedicated butler service and access to incredible marine reserves just offshore.

Why couples love St. Lucia
  • Direct flights from many East Coast hubs (under 4 hours)
  • UNESCO Biosphere Reserve — pristine and protected nature
  • Some of the best scuba diving in the Caribbean
  • Dramatically less crowded than Aruba, Turks & Caicos, or Cancún

Best Honeymoon Destinations by Season

The five destinations above are our headline picks, but the right honeymoon starts with your wedding date. Weather, crowds, and room availability change dramatically from month to month, and a place that is perfect in March can be rainy, scorching, or half-closed in another season. Here is how we match destinations to the calendar for couples marrying in late 2026 and 2027.

Wedding monthWhere it shinesKeep in mind
December–AprilMaldives, Caribbean (including St. Lucia), Mexico, Costa RicaPeak season for warm-weather escapes. The best villas and overwater rooms go first, especially around the holidays and spring break.
April–JuneSantorini, Amalfi Coast, Hawaii, BaliEurope’s sweet spot before the summer rush. Bali’s dry season begins in April.
June–OctoberFrench Polynesia, Fiji, Bali, safari in Kenya or TanzaniaThe South Pacific’s drier months and classic safari season. July and August are busy and expensive in the Mediterranean.
September–OctoberSantorini, Amalfi Coast, Bali, safariWarm seas and thinner crowds in Europe. This is also the heart of Atlantic hurricane season, so we steer Caribbean couples carefully.
NovemberMexico, Hawaii, Bali, and the start of high season in the Caribbean as hurricane season endsMany smaller Amalfi Coast hotels and ferries close for the winter, so Italy works better as a city honeymoon this month.

Winter weddings (December–February)

Winter is the Maldives’ moment. The northeast monsoon, generally December through April, brings the sunniest, calmest stretch of the year, which is exactly when overwater villas are most in demand. Closer to home, St. Lucia and the rest of the Caribbean are in their dry, breezy high season. If you are marrying near Christmas or New Year’s, plan early: holiday weeks are the hardest dates of the year to secure at top resorts.

Spring weddings (March–May)

Spring is our favorite season for Europe. Late April and May bring warm days to Santorini and the Amalfi Coast without the July crowds. Bali’s dry season runs roughly April through October, so a May wedding lines up beautifully with a jungle-villa honeymoon. Couples drawn to Japan should read our guide to cherry blossom season, one of the most romantic and most quickly booked windows in travel.

Summer weddings (June–August)

Summer is the South Pacific’s best season. In French Polynesia and Fiji, the drier, less humid months fall in the middle of the year, and calm lagoons make for spectacular snorkeling. It is also prime time for a safari honeymoon in East Africa; our first African safari guide explains how to pair a few days in the bush with a beach finale. The Mediterranean is gorgeous but at its busiest and priciest, so we often nudge summer couples toward a late-September Europe trip as a delayed honeymoon instead.

Fall weddings (September–November)

September and early October are wonderful in Santorini and along the Amalfi Coast, with warm water and fewer crowds. Bali is still in its dry season. The Atlantic hurricane season officially runs June 1 through November 30 and peaks in early-to-mid September, so for a fall Caribbean honeymoon we choose islands, resorts, and travel insurance with that in mind, or point you toward Bali or the Mediterranean instead.

What Drives the Cost of a Honeymoon

We do not publish one-size-fits-all price tags, because two couples going to the same island can spend very different amounts. What we can tell you is where the money goes, so you can decide where to splurge and where to save.

  • Season: The same villa can cost far more in peak weeks than in shoulder season. Shifting your dates by even a few weeks can free up room in the budget.
  • Room category: In the Maldives, French Polynesia, and the Caribbean, the jump from a garden room to an overwater villa or a suite with a private pool is usually the single biggest line item. Often it is worth it; sometimes a beach villa delivers 90 percent of the magic.
  • Getting there: Long-haul flights to the Indian Ocean or South Pacific, plus seaplane or domestic transfers, add up quickly, and lie-flat business class seats can rival the hotel bill. Shorter flights to the Caribbean and Mexico leave more of the budget for the stay itself.
  • Meal plans: All-inclusive or full-board plans make sense on remote islands where every meal is at your resort. In Italy, Greece, and Bali, eating out is part of the fun, so we usually book breakfast only.

In general terms, the Caribbean and Mexico give you the most honeymoon for the travel time, Bali offers remarkable villa value once you are there, and the Maldives, French Polynesia, and the Seychelles sit at the top end. Flight Times from the East Coast

From Greensboro and the rest of the East Coast, travel time is the most underrated part of honeymoon planning. After months of wedding logistics, a 30-hour journey can feel very different from a 4-hour hop. Here is a realistic picture:

DestinationTypical routingTravel feel
Caribbean & MexicoNonstop from many East Coast hubs; some smaller islands need a connectionEasy. Same day, minimal jet lag.
Italy & GreeceOvernight flight to Europe, then a connection to Naples or SantoriniModerate. Plan a gentle first day.
HawaiiUsually a connection on the West CoastA full travel day, but no passport needed.
French PolynesiaConnect in Los Angeles; nonstop LAX–Papeete flights run about 8½ hoursLong, but simpler than most couples expect.
FijiConnect on the West Coast; Fiji Airways’ nonstop LAX–Nadi flight runs about 11 hoursLong. Overnight flights help.
MaldivesNo nonstop from the US; connect through a Middle East or European hubVery long. Worth a stopover night.
BaliOne or two connections through Asia or the Middle EastVery long. Plan at least 10 nights.

For the longest journeys, we often build in a stopover night en route, choose flights that land in time for an easy first evening, and keep the first day free of plans. If you are traveling the day after your wedding, a shorter flight for the first leg is a kindness to yourselves.

Honeymoon Perks Your Advisor Can Request

Hotels love honeymooners, but the best touches usually come from asking the right way, well in advance. When we book your honeymoon, these are the requests we routinely make on your behalf. Nothing here is guaranteed, and availability varies by property, but asking costs nothing.

  • A welcome amenity: Champagne, a celebration cake, or a handwritten note from the resort.
  • Room upgrades: Requested in advance and confirmed when available, often more achievable outside peak weeks.
  • Early check-in or late checkout: Especially valuable after an overnight flight or before a late departure.
  • Partner amenities: Through our preferred partner properties, extras such as resort credits or daily breakfast may be included.
  • A private dinner: A table on the beach, on a dock, or in your villa, arranged before you arrive.
  • The surprise room setup Elizabeth describes above, timed for your first night.

One practical note: some resorts ask for proof of your wedding date to honor honeymoon offers, and many limit them to trips taken within a set window after the wedding. We check the fine print before you go.

Registry Tips: Letting Guests Gift the Honeymoon

Many couples would rather have a sunset sail than a second stand mixer, and your guests often feel the same. A few ideas that work well:

  • Register for experiences, not just a lump sum. “Couples’ massage in Bali” or “private boat day on the Amalfi Coast” gives guests something specific and joyful to give.
  • Lock in the trip first. Book and pay the deposit on the core of your honeymoon before the wedding, then let registry gifts fund the upgrades and extras. That way your plans never depend on what comes in.
  • Consider travel gift cards. Destinara gift cards can be applied toward a honeymoon, which is an easy option for guests who prefer a traditional gift.

Your 2027 Honeymoon Planning Timeline

  • 9–12 months out: Choose your destination and book the hotel, especially overwater villas, small boutique properties, and holiday or peak-season dates.
  • 6–9 months out: Book flights and add travel insurance. We generally suggest Faye first, with Allianz as another strong option; our guide Do You Really Need Travel Insurance? explains why.
  • 3–6 months out: Check passports. Indonesia, for example, requires at least six months of validity beyond your arrival date and two blank pages, and Bali charges foreign visitors a tourist levy. The European Union has also said its new ETIAS travel authorization for visa-exempt visitors, including Americans, is scheduled to begin in the last quarter of 2026, so check its status before a 2027 trip to Greece or Italy.
  • 1–3 months out: Reserve dinners, spa treatments, and private excursions, and finalize your surprise requests.
💍

A note on names: If one of you is changing your name, book your honeymoon under the name that appears on the passport you will actually travel with. Airline tickets must match your travel document, and updating a passport right before the wedding is cutting it close.

Honeymoon Planning FAQ

When should we start planning a 2027 honeymoon?

As soon as your wedding date is set. Nine to twelve months gives you the best choice of rooms, especially for peak-season weddings and in-demand villas. Shorter timelines can still work beautifully; they simply call for more flexibility.

How long should a honeymoon be?

For the Caribbean or Mexico, five to seven nights is a lovely length. For Europe, plan at least a week. For the Maldives, Bali, French Polynesia, or Fiji, we recommend ten nights or more so the travel time is worth it.

Should we leave right after the wedding or wait?

Either works. Some couples take a short “minimoon” close to home right after the wedding and save the big trip for the ideal season at their dream destination. If your wedding falls during a destination’s rainy or hurricane season, waiting a few months can be the smarter choice.

What is the best honeymoon within a short flight of the East Coast?

St. Lucia is our favorite for drama and romance, and the wider Caribbean and Mexico offer a wide range of adults-only and villa resorts, most just a few hours away.
